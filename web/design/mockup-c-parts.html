A parts list, not a platform

rho is the harness, unbundled.

A coding agent harness in Rust. Ten small crates. Take the parts you want. Replace any part with your own crate.

One idea

You do not fork rho. You compose it.

Most harnesses ship as one bundle. To change one part, you fork the whole. rho inverts that. The agent loop knows nothing about HTTP or terminals. Providers, tools, and frontends plug into traits.

$ cargo build -p rho-cli --no-default-features --features minimal

One measurement

A session should cost megabytes, not hundreds.

At 50 parallel sessions, a 100 MB harness needs 5 GB of RAM. rho treats memory and start time as product features. The rho row stays empty until our benchmarks produce real values.

Two tiers

Extend it in Rust, or in any language.

Tier 1Compiled traits

Implement Provider, Tool, or Hook in your own crate.

Link it into your build. Zero overhead.

Tier 2Out-of-process plugins

Ship a subprocess that speaks stdio JSON-RPC. Any language.

A crashed plugin does not take down the session.

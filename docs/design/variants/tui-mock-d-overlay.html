rho TUI — direction D, «Overlay»

The thesis: a panel is a moment, not a layout. The user opens a panel on purpose, reads it, and closes it in seconds. So a panel does not fight the transcript for the 14 rows the band reserves. It takes a tall transient region, sized to its content, above a composer that stays pinned. The band grows for the moment and shrinks back. The resting layout is the baseline, unchanged: 14 rows, no alternate screen. The cost is a seam — see the frame named the seam. This direction is allowed to lose, and its frames state the price out loud.

rho TUI — direction B, Breath

The thesis: the band takes the rows it needs, and no more. The baseline band is 14 rows, always, and the idle frame is 57% blank. Here the band rests at 4 rows: rule, ❯, rule, footer. It grows while a turn runs, one row per live row. The cap is the terminal height minus four, so 20 rows on an 80×24 terminal. Four rows of the terminal's own scrollback always stay in view, so the band never becomes the alternate screen by stealth. The band never shrinks while a turn streams, because a shrink under moving text reads as flicker. It shrinks at two moments only: the turn ends, or you close a panel. Each frame below states its own height, so the switcher shows the breath. The colours are the six theme roles as theme.rs resolves them.
